SAT Messenger Privacy Policy

How SAT Limited collects, processes, protects, and puts you in control of your information across SAT Messenger (com.aistudio.satmessenger.satlim).

Effective: November 27, 2026 Version 4.2.07.11.2026.14.5.1 (Build 5) Zero-Trust Cloud Firestore

1. Data Usage

What information is processed and why it is needed to operate SAT Messenger

Account & Directory Profile Identity

  • Google Identity Sign-In: Authenticates your account via Jetpack CredentialManager and Firebase Authentication, receiving your UID, display name, email address, and profile photo.
  • Profile & Usernames: Stores your display name, primary @username (email prefix), optional secondary @username, optional phone number, status message, avatar, live availability state, and lastSeen timestamp in /users/{userId} so colleagues and friends can find and reach you.

Messaging, Media, Voice & Communities Collaboration

  • Conversations & Broadcasts: Direct chats, group rooms, channels, communities, emoji reactions, read receipts, typing flags, and 24-hour status stories are synced via Cloud Firestore solely to deliver real-time communication to authorized participants.
  • Media & Icons: Profile photos, group/channel/community icons, chat attachments, and status media selected through the Android Photo Picker are compressed on-device and synced to authorized conversation or group members.

Device Contacts Discovery (Find Friends) Opt-In

  • Explicit User Action Only: Device contacts (ContactsContract) are scanned only after you grant READ_CONTACTS permission and tap Find in the Contacts directory.
  • Minimal Correlation: Normalized phone numbers and email addresses are matched against registered SAT Messenger users to display existing friends on your device; unmatched personal contacts are never uploaded or stored in Cloud Firestore.

SAT Limited AI & Update Checks Services

  • SAT Limited AI: Prompts, selected modes (Smart, Fast, Reasoning), optional Google Search grounding queries, and user-selected images are sent through Firebase AI Logic exclusively when you interact with the assistant.
  • In-App Version Check: Checking for updates in Profile & Settings fetches public release metadata from GitHub Releases and compares your installed versionName locally.

2. Security Practices

Technical and organizational safeguards protecting enterprise communications

Zero-Trust Firestore Rules

  • All database reads and writes require verified Google authentication (anonymous authentication is strictly disabled).
  • Private subcollections (contacts, settings/privacy, blocked) are strictly scoped to the owning userId.
  • Group rooms, channels, and communities enforce membership checks, creator protection, and admin-only role/icon updates server-side.

App Check & Encrypted Transport

  • Requests to Firebase AI Logic and backend services are attested using Firebase App Check to prevent unauthorized API abuse.
  • No API secrets are hardcoded in application source code; all network communication with Firebase and GitHub uses HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit.

Least-Privilege Architecture

  • Zero broad storage permissions: photo and video selection uses Android's zero-permission PickVisualMedia picker.
  • Abuse and compliance reports are isolated inside a restricted /reports collection for authorized governance review.

3. User Rights & Controls

Manage visibility, customize data, revoke access, or delete your account anytime

Presence, Receipts & Profile Control

  • Privacy Toggles: Enable or disable Read Receipts and Online & Last Seen Presence anytime under Profile & Settings → Privacy Controls.
  • Edit or Remove Data: Change or remove your profile photo, phone number, availability status, and secondary @username (up to 3 edits per active day with a 10-day cooldown); admins may update or remove group, channel, and community icons at any time.

Blocking, History Clearing & Erasure

  • Safety Controls: Block or unblock contacts, clear message history, delete chats, or submit compliance reports directly from conversations and Profile settings.
  • Account Deletion: Permanently delete your SAT Messenger account, profile record, and private settings immediately via Profile & Settings → Delete Account & Clear Profile.

4. Android Permission Transparency

Only permissions directly tied to user-initiated features are declared
Permission Requirement Purpose & Revocation
INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Install-time Real-time Cloud Firestore sync, Google authentication, SAT Limited AI, and GitHub Release version checks.
READ_CONTACTS Runtime (Opt-In) Optional Find Friends device contact correlation; revocable anytime in Android App Settings.
RECORD_AUDIO Runtime (Opt-In) Requested only when tapping the microphone to record voice notes; revocable anytime in Android App Settings.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Runtime (Android 13+) Posts incoming message alerts to the Notification Center; toggleable in-app or in system settings.
